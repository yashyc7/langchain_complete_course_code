Text splitting is the process of breaking large chunks of text (like articles,pdf , html pages or books  )
into smaller pieces( chunks)
that an LLM can handle effectively. 

-> overcoming model limitations : many embedding models and language models have maximum input size 
contraints allowing us to process documents that would otherwise exceed these limits. 


-> optimizing computational resources : working with smaller chunks of text that can be more memory-efficient and allows for better parallelization of processing tasks.

1. What is a Text Splitter (first principles)

A TextSplitter is a preprocessing component in LangChain that:

• Takes raw text or documents
• Breaks them into smaller chunks
• Preserves context using overlap
• Produces chunks suitable for embedding, vector storage, and retrieval

Why this is mandatory:

LLMs and embedding models have context length limits.
Vector databases perform semantic search on chunks, not entire documents.
Poor chunking = poor retrieval = hallucinations.


text splitters improve the quality of responses from llm since they hallucinate with larger texts
